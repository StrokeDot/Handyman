# Changes

## 0.20.0
- **More natural skin**: the hand is less orange, with a soft natural sheen like real skin, knuckles and fingertips a little redder where the skin is thin, creases a little darker, light glowing through the edges of the fingers, and fine pores up close.
- **The sleeve looks like real suede**: a soft velvety sheen on the folds, patches where the nap lies differently, darker inside the folds and worn a little lighter on the edges that stick out, with the nap's fine texture. A sleeve picture of your own keeps the folds, stitches and wear.

## 0.19.0
- **Natural fingers, no more hooks**: each finger now bends the way a real finger does (before, the joints bent a little sideways, so the hand looked like a claw), the fingers lie together instead of splayed, and a relaxed hand is gently curved. Holding a page, the hand pinches it between the thumb and the side of the index fingertip, like paper, while the other fingers stay loose instead of curling into a fist. The holding hand's fingers lie nearly flat on the book.
- **Left hand under the book, like Arthur Morgan**: new "Left hand" choice under the hand options. "On the book" is the book on a table with the left hand holding its corner; "Under the book" has the left hand holding the book up from beneath, palm up, like Arthur reading his journal - under the closed book from the left, then sliding under the left half as the cover opens (no table shadow under a book held up).
- **The hand waits below the book during a pause**: when there is a pause between turns of about a second or more, the right hand goes back down below the book, waits, and comes back in time for the next page - always on the timing you set.
- **Hand size**: new "Hand size" setting (60 % to 140 %). 100 % is a real man's hand next to the book at its real size.
- On fast flips the wrist eases straight as the hand lets go of each page.

## 0.18.0
- **Both hands, like Arthur Morgan**: the left hand now holds the book too. While the book is closed it steadies the cover as the strap is undone; it moves aside to the left before the cover swings over, then holds the open book by its bottom-left corner, below the pages, so turned pages never land on it. It eases up a little as each page comes down, and moves aside whenever the right hand comes near. On a very small book, where two hands would not fit, it rests on the table beside the book instead. Untick "...and the left hand holds the book" (Animation tab, under the hand option) to show only the right hand.
- **Smoother hand movement**: no more sudden flip of the hand when it lays the cover or a page down - it now grips the page so that, letting go, it only has to roll a little onto the page's back. It lets go just past upright, and every change of movement (taking hold, letting go, setting off for the next page) flows on instead of starting or stopping dead. On fast flips it simply lets go and lifts away instead of rushing to lay each page.
- Hand distances are worked out in real centimetres, so the hands behave the same on small and large books.

## 0.17.0
- **Journals can have a strap**: in Change book design, "Book kind" is Book or Journal (a diary). Only a journal can have "Leather strap round the fore-edge": a strap in the cover's leather from the back cover round to the front, like Arthur Morgan's journal. When the video starts closed, the strap comes undone just before the cover opens - and if the hand is shown, the hand pinches its tip, lifts it off and folds it out, then opens the cover. (The strap is in the rendered video; the live view does not show it yet.)

## 0.16.1
- **The hand opens the book** (with "Start closed, then open the cover"): like Arthur Morgan, it catches the closed cover by its top corner, swings it open past upright, lets go, smooths it down on the left, and then goes on to turn the pages.

## 0.16.0
- **Change the hand's look, like the book's**: under "Show a 3D hand turning the pages" (Animation tab), "Hand skin" can be the bare hand, a leather glove made from the book cover's own leather, or any picture of your own (leather, fabric, skin) wrapped over the hand. "Sleeve" can be the brown suede or any fabric picture, repeated over the sleeve with its stitching and folds kept.

## 0.15.1
- **The page leads, the hand follows**: the hand now holds the page's edge between thumb and fingers and goes wherever the page goes, at the page's own speed (set by the turn time). Around its grip the hand turns like fingers do on paper, so the wrist stays natural; it stands upright with the thumb up while the page stands, lets go once the page is past upright, and smooths it down as it lands.

## 0.15.0
- **The hand turns pages like in Red Dead Redemption 2**: it slides in low to the page's edge, catches it, and carries the page all the way over, turning with it - flat on the right page, upright with the thumb up while the page stands, flat again as it lays the page down. Then it smooths the page with an open hand and goes back for the next one.
- The wrist can now bend further, so the hand stays on the page through the whole turn.

## 0.14.1
- **Cleaner, healthier skin on the hand**: no more scaly, cracked look or blotches; the knuckles and veins are softer, the colour is even, and light passes through the skin a little more, closer to a game like Red Dead Redemption 2.

## 0.14.0
- **A 3D hand turns the pages**: tick "Show a 3D hand turning the pages" (Animation tab). A right hand in a brown suede sleeve reaches in from the front right, pinches each page by its bottom corner, carries it over, lets go and moves on to the next page. It comes in before the first turn and goes back out after the last. Pages in a fast flip turn on their own.
- **Real size**: the hand is a real hand's size, so on a small book it looks big and on a big book small, and the arm bends naturally at the elbow and wrist from any camera angle.
- **Looks like the reference photos**: the skin is painted from photos of a real hand (knuckles, veins, nails, hair at the wrist), and the sleeve's folds were made by draping real cloth over the arm.
- The hand is in the rendered video and in "Preview still". It does not show in the live view yet.

## 0.12.1

Before, they kept their width, so on a shorter page they looked too big and could run off the edge.

## 0.12.0
- **A clearer Pages panel**: the book is shown as it opens, one row per open spread ("Inside cover - page 1", "Pages 2 - 3", ...), each with a small picture of both pages and what is on them. Double-click a row to put pictures or text on those pages; right-click (or "This page") to move a page earlier or later, make it blank or remove it.
- **Add pages** menu, in plain words: 2 blank pages (one more sheet), pages from pictures (each picture fills a whole page), or a page of text. New pages go right after the last page that has something on it.
- **The book always has all its pages**: "Sheets of paper" sets how many (2 pages per sheet), blank pages fill the rest, and the book grows by itself if you add more. ("Fill with blank" is gone - it is automatic now.)
- **Page turns in the video**: choose how many pages turn (fewer = a shorter video); the book keeps all its pages.
- **Text in StrokeScribe's fonts**: "Add text..." in the pictures window (and "A page of text..."). Pick a font from StrokeScribe's list - fonts you already downloaded in StrokeScribe are ready here, others download once with their licence and then work in both apps - or use a font file of your own. Size in points (12 pt reads like a printed book), colour, alignment and line spacing, with a preview on your paper. Text can be moved, turned and edited again like a picture (double-click it in the list).
- **Page numbers in the live view**: a counter in the corner says which pages are open ("Pages 6-7", "Turning page 5 -> pages 6-7"), and the open spread is marked in the Pages list while it plays. It never appears in the video; "Page numbers" under the timeline turns it off.

## 0.11.4
- **Sharp at any angle**: the live view now uses anisotropic filtering, so covers and pages seen at a slant stay sharp instead of blurring, and covers and pages are loaded at a higher resolution. (The final render was always sharp.)
- **Cover edges match the cover**: the edges of the cover boards (the thickness of the cover) and the inside of the spine now take their colour from the edge of your cover picture, so a blue cover gets blue edges. In Change book design, "Cover edges" can switch back to the leather image as it is.
- **Thickness**: in Change book design, "Cover thickness" (the boards, 4.2 mm on the Leather Journal) and "Paper thickness" (one sheet, 0.45 mm). The number of pages also changes how thick the book is. A very thick book is limited so the turning pages never touch (checked for every turn with thin and thick covers and paper).

## 0.11.3
- **The cover follows your picture's shape**: the 3D cover's corners are now cut as round as the corners of your cover picture (read from its transparent pixels), in the live view as well as the render. Before, the live view drew the cover as a square box and the picture's transparent corners showed as a black square.
- Where the cover still sits under a transparent pixel (for example the corners on the spine side), it shows the colour next to it instead of black.

## 0.11.2
- **Fixed: the book disappearing from the live view.** A small memory of page shapes sometimes emptied itself right after storing a page; the drawing then stopped halfway, and each failed frame left the graphics one step deeper until it overflowed and nothing could be drawn. The memory is fixed, and any failed frame now cleans up after itself so the view always recovers. The live view log no longer grows without end (it had reached 79 MB).
- **True shape** button (above the live view): the book seen flat from straight above, with no perspective - its real proportions. Only for checking the shape; the video still uses your camera.
- **Save my camera...** (Camera tab): keep a camera under your own name; it appears in "Start from" (marked with a star) for every book. Delete removes it.
- **Zoom by** (Camera tab): "Lens" (the book keeps its look, only its size changes) or "Move the camera closer" (stronger 3D, the near end looks bigger - the look before 0.10.2).

## 0.11.1
- **The pages meet in the middle again**: 0.11.0 sewed the left and right pages on separate halves of the spine, which opened a dark gap in the middle of the book. Now the spine's crest travels through the book as you read, always between the two pages you see, so they meet at the middle, and the pages under them still never cut through each other (checked for every page turn).
- **One picture window for the whole book**: "Pictures on the pages..." (or double-click a page) shows the two open pages side by side, like the book. "Previous pages" / "Next pages" (or Page Up / Page Down) go through the book, and the title says which pages you are on. Pictures already on a page show up here and can be moved: a picture over the middle becomes a picture across both pages, and moving it back onto one side makes it that page's picture again. The list says which page each picture is on.
- **Copy, paste and undo**: Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy a picture, also onto other pages; Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo; Delete removes the selected picture.
- "+ Images..." is now "+ Full-page images..." (it adds new pages at the end of the book, each image a whole page).

## 0.11.0
- **Cover size apart from the page size**: in Change book design, untick "Cover follows the pages" and set the cover width and height yourself. The cover can never be smaller than the pages; the preview, the 3D book, the camera framing and the required image sizes all follow.
- **A picture across two pages**: select a page and press "Picture across two pages...". One picture covers the whole open spread; the left half stays on the left page and the right half on the right page, so each half turns with its own page, like a printed double page. It fades out a hair at the spine (the gutter).
- **Margins** in the picture window: a blue dashed line shows the area kept clear around the page, "Fit inside the margins" sizes a picture to it, new pictures start fitted inside it, and the window tells you the pixel size to draw a picture that fills it.
- **Pages no longer cut through each other at the spine**: each stack is now sewn on its own half of the spine's hump, with the top page nearest the crest. Before, the first pages were sewn below their neighbours and crossed them at the gutter, so a lower page's drawing showed through as dots near the spine.
- Opening Change book design and saving without changing the size no longer resizes your images by a pixel.

## 0.10.0
- **Pictures on a page, like layers in After Effects**: select a page and press "Pictures on this page..." (or double-click the page). Add as many pictures as you like, drag each one to move it, mouse wheel to make it bigger or smaller, Shift + wheel to turn it. A preview shows the closed and open book in the new shape, with the current shape as a dashed gold line, before anything is saved. Your cover, spine and paper images can be stretched, cropped to fill, or adjusted one by one; the required image sizes follow the new shape.
- After a shape change, page images that no longer fit the page can be adjusted for you (crop all, or one by one) or kept stretched.
- Checked: every page shape, turn style and 10 / 25 / 50 sheets with wide, square and tall books (no page touches another), in the live view and the render.

## 0.9.0 - page styles and faster renders
- **Page shape** (Book section): Flat, Curved or Very curved. Curved pages rise out of the spine in a soft arch, like a real open book. Every page stays on top of the one below it.
- **Page turn style** (Animation tab): Natural (the smooth arc you had), Corner peel (like a hand-made After Effects turn: the bottom corner curls up slowly, the page whips over, then lands with its corner still curled) and Quick and light.
- **Add a fast flip...** (Animation tab): flip through many pages in a second or two, like riffling them with your thumb. Pages fan over without passing through each other; blank pages are added if the book needs more.
- **Faster renders**: frames where nothing moves are copied instead of rendered again. Photoreal keeps the scene loaded between frames, cleans up noise on the graphics card and stops sampling pixels that are already clean (about 30% faster even without a graphics card).
- **Render with 2 Blenders at once** (Settings, on by default): each renders half of the video at the same time, so your graphics card is kept busy instead of waiting.
- Thin books: the bound edges of the pages are now always at least a sheet apart at the spine.

## 0.5.0
- Live 3D view: the book, your pages, the cover opening and the page turns play inside the app instantly, no rendering. Play / pause, speed (0.25× to 2×), loop, and a timeline you can drag.
- Drag the live view with the mouse to move the camera; every setting change shows immediately. Blender is only needed for the final video.
- "Book opens at": opening from the beginning now swings only the cover, with the pages staying on the right (no more half of the book lifting like a slab).
- Pages on each side curve according to how thick that side is.

## 0.4.0
- Move the camera with the mouse: in "Set up the shot", drag to look around the book, right-drag to move it, mouse wheel to zoom, double-click to reset.
- Pages curve down into the spine like a real open book.
- Thicker book with a paper block that follows the page curve; page edges show stacked sheets.
- Leather: fine pebbled grain and a faint waxed sheen instead of cloudy blotches.

## 0.3.0
- New Camera panel: start from Eye level, Top-down, Angled or Low angle, then adjust tilt, turn, picture tilt, perspective, size and position.
- Live outline of the shot that updates while you drag, before any rendering.
- Slow camera moves: push-in, pull-out or sideways drift.
- The camera frames the book automatically from any angle.

## 0.2.1
- More room above the book, so a page rising mid-turn stays inside the frame.
- Unticked boxes now look clearly unticked.

## 0.2.0
- Book templates: leather colour, paper colour, age and burnt edges, stitching, spine bands, page shape, emblem (medallion or pressed into the leather) and cover title. Make copies to create new books.
- The book can start closed: the cover swings open and the book slides to the centre.
- The page corner lifts first, then the page turns.
- Save and open projects (.hmproj).
- Windows installer with automatic update checks.
